Important limits

This starter supports text overlays, adding PNG/JPG images or signature images, rotating/deleting pages, inserting blank pages, merging a second PDF and downloading. It does not yet edit existing text in place, OCR scanned documents, redact content securely, guarantee every form field, compress files, or apply cryptographic digital signatures. It loads PDF libraries from a CDN, so internet access is required. Do not rely on visual white boxes to securely hide confidential text.
